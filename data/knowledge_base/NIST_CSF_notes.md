# NIST Cybersecurity Framework — Prototype Notes
Source: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework

NIST CSF organizes cybersecurity outcomes around Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover. A detection assistant can use these functions to structure recommendations and incident-response context.

Production deployments should ingest the official NIST publications rather than relying on this short summary.
